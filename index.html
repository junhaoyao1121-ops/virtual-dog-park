Dog Encyclopedia

Beagle

Size: Small to medium.

Personality: Curious and friendly.

Everyday care: Provide daily walks and check its ears regularly.

Golden Retriever

Size: Medium to large.

Personality: Gentle and energetic.

Everyday care: Provide daily exercise and brush its coat.

Poodle

Size: Varies by type.

Personality: Intelligent and active.

Everyday care: Provide exercise and regular coat grooming.
